How it works

1 · SignalsEach day, after the close, every strategy reads the latest prices and decides its position.
2 · Simulated fillsOrders fill at the next open with fees and slippage; 2% stop-losses are checked intraday.
3 · Permanent recordResults are appended to a ledger that is never rewritten, so nothing can be tidied up later.
4 · AutomatedA scheduled GitHub Actions job runs the update and rebuilds this page daily.
Educational project. Backtested and simulated (paper) results only: no real money is traded, past performance does not predict future results, and nothing here is investment advice. Data: Yahoo Finance daily bars. · Source code
